05 For opticians · in store testing

ARCO P++D
Measure, show and fit spectacles on one tablet.

An app for opticians. It measures what the lens lab needs (PD, pantoscopic tilt and frame tilt), shows the patient how different lenses look in real scenes, and lets them try frames on in 3D with the front camera. On iPhone, iPad and Android.

PD · Pantoscopic tilt · Frame tiltLens demos3D try-oniPhone · iPad · Android
How measuring works
01 · Measurements

PD, pantoscopic tilt and frame tilt

The app places the points on the photo, the optician checks them and can drag any point into place. Results can be exported.

02 · Lens demos

Real scenes, real lenses

Office, outdoor, water, day and night driving, golf, skiing and blue light, through single vision, progressive and personalised designs, AR, polarised, photochromic and blue filter.

03 · Frame selection

Every frame in 3D

Browse frames with sizes (like 52□18-140) and colours, spin and zoom each one, and carry the choice into the lens demos and results.

04 · 3D try-on

See it on your face

The patient sees themself wearing the frame in 3D at true size as the head turns. Switch frame, colour, tint and coatings live, and compare up to four snapshots.

Lens demos

Upgrades are easier to explain when the patient can see them.

A slider compares a standard and a premium lens side by side in the same scene, and Eyewear shows how thick the lens will be in the chosen frame.

How measuring works

Two ways to measure, depending on the device.

With a depth sensor

On an iPhone or iPad with LiDAR or a TrueDepth camera, the app reads true distances from the sensor.

With the marker frame

On any other device the patient wears the light 3D-printed frame above. Its printed markers give the app the scale and angle of each photo, so an ordinary camera can measure.

Questions

About ARCO

What is ARCO?

ARCO is the family of software products built and run by Branding Catalyst. ARCO Optics (Arco Labs, Arco Wholesale and Arco Retail) connects optical labs, distributors and stores, and ARCO P++D helps opticians measure and fit spectacles on one tablet, and ARCO Communication is a WhatsApp CRM for Shopify brands.

Who are the ARCO Optics products for?

Arco Labs is for optical laboratories with one or many branches, Arco Wholesale is for lens, frame and eyewear distributors, and Arco Retail is a phone app for optical stores. They work on their own or connected together.

Are they built for Indian businesses?

Yes. Billing is GST-ready (CGST, SGST and IGST), updates and purchase orders go out on WhatsApp, payments can use Razorpay links, and Arco Retail keeps working without internet.

Can ARCO be customised for my business?

Yes. Because we build ARCO ourselves, we can adapt workflows, reports and integrations for your team. Book a demo and tell us how you work today.

What is ARCO P++D?

An app for opticians that measures pupillary distance, pantoscopic tilt and frame tilt, shows patients how different lenses look in real scenes, and lets them try frames on in 3D. It runs on iPhone, iPad and Android, and is being tested in optical stores.

How do I get a demo or pricing?

Use the “Book a demo” button for the product you are interested in, or message us on WhatsApp. We will show you the product on a short call and share pricing for your size of business.
