Questions

Before you ask

How long does an ERP take?

Most ERPs go live in 8–12 weeks. We launch the most important module first, usually inventory or billing, so your team starts using it early, then add the rest in phases.

Can it work with Tally or our existing software?

Yes. We can sync with Tally and other accounting tools, or move your data across, depending on what suits your team. We plan this on the scoping call.

Why custom instead of a ready ERP?

Ready ERPs make you change how you work and charge per user every year. A custom ERP fits your process, has no per-user licence fee, and you own it.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the modules and users. After a free 30-minute scoping call we give you a fixed quote, and you pay per milestone only after you accept it.
