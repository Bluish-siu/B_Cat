FAQ

Questions about our services.

What does "AI-first" actually mean for my project?

We use AI at every stage: to plan scope and timelines, to generate and test repetitive code and content, and to make your brand visible in AI search. People still lead the strategy, design and quality checks, and an engineer reviews every release.

Can I hire you for just one service?

Yes. Many clients start with one service, such as a website or an AEO audit, and add more later. Bundles are optional.

Who do you work with?

Startups, growing businesses and established brands across India and abroad, from cafés and clinics to manufacturers and SaaS companies.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. Ask ARC or use the idea checker for a first estimate; after a free 30-minute scoping call we give you a fixed quote.

Do you maintain what you build?

Yes. We offer monitoring, updates and care plans after launch, plus monthly AEO, GEO and growth work to keep improving results.

Not sure which service you need?

Tell ARC what you want to achieve, or share a few details and we'll recommend the right mix.
