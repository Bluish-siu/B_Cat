FAQ

About the checker.

How accurate is the estimate?

It is a rough first estimate based on the features you describe, so real projects can differ. A person on our team reviews it before sending a price, and we confirm a fixed price and timeline after a free scoping call.

Is my idea kept private?

Your idea is only sent to us if you ask for your price. We use it to prepare your scoping call and never share it. We are happy to sign an NDA before you share more detail.

Why do you ask for my details?

A price only means something once a person has checked your plan. Your details let us send you a price range on WhatsApp and follow up with a fixed quote. We never sell or share them.

My idea is unusual. Can you still build it?

Probably. If the checker misses something, describe it on a call. We build AI agents, ERPs, mobile apps, 3D experiences and much more.

Prefer to talk it through?

Book a free 30-minute scoping call. Bring your idea, we bring a plan.
