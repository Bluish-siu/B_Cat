Brands, products and AI, built by one team.

Branding Catalyst is a studio based in Mumbai. We shape how a business looks, sounds and gets found, then build the apps, ERP systems, websites and AI that run it, all with Catalyst Engine, our AI-powered way of working.

Our story

Where brand
meets build.

Most growing businesses end up managing three separate partners: an agency for the brand, a development shop for the software, and a marketing team for growth. Each works to its own brief and timeline, and the business is left joining the pieces together.

We started Branding Catalyst to remove that gap. One team owns the whole journey, from the name and logo to the mobile app, the ERP behind it, and the content that brings customers in.

AI changed how fast that journey can move. Catalyst Engine combines leading AI models with our playbooks and ready-made modules, so our engineers spend their time on what makes your product different. Every release is still reviewed by a person before it ships.

Search is changing too. Customers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity what to buy, so we build every brand and website to be found and recommended by AI, through AEO and GEO, from day one.

And you can talk to all of it through ARC, our AI assistant, who turns an idea into a plan in seconds.

Principles

What guides
every project.

Four rules we hold ourselves to, whether we are designing a logo or launching an ERP for a factory floor.

01

Brand comes first

Every screen, message and feature should feel like the same company. We set the identity and voice before we write code.

02

Show, don't promise

A working demo every week beats a long status report. You see real progress and can steer early.

03

Pay for what works

Fixed scope and price, and payment per milestone only after you accept it. Our incentives match yours.

04

Built to be found

A great product nobody finds is wasted. We design for Google and for AI answers from the first page.
