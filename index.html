FAQ

Questions we
hear most.

Quick answers about Branding Catalyst, Catalyst Engine, ARC, pricing and timelines.

What is Branding Catalyst?

An AI-first product and growth studio. We build AI products, Android and iOS apps, ERP systems, websites and 3D web experiences, and we help brands get recommended by AI search through AEO and GEO. Founded in 2019, we are based in Mumbai and led by our founder, Abhishek Trivedi, with 50+ projects delivered for national and international clients.

What is Catalyst Engine?

Our build system. It sits on top of leading AI models and adds our playbooks, ready modules and AI agents that plan, code, test and release. Engineers review every release.

Who is ARC?

ARC is the voice of Catalyst Engine. Tell him your idea in plain words and he replies with features, a plan and a timeline. Our team then confirms scope and price with you.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) makes your content the direct answer in AI assistants and voice search. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) gets tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to mention and recommend your brand.

What is RAG?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation lets an AI assistant answer from your own documents and data, with sources, so its answers stay accurate.

Do you build Android and iOS apps?

Yes. We usually build both from one codebase, which saves time and cost, and publish to the Play Store and App Store.

How much does it cost?

It depends on scope. Use the idea checker or ask ARC for a first estimate. We give a fixed quote after a free scoping call.

Ready to launch something new?

Book a free 30-minute call. Bring the rough idea; we will bring a plan, a price range and a first sketch of the product.
